Ruohang Feng ([@Vonng](https://github.com/Vonng)) writes **SOW** and
[Pigsty](https://pigsty.io/), the PostgreSQL distribution it builds repositories for.

SOW started from a delivery problem rather than a packaging one. Pigsty ships
hundreds of RPM and DEB packages across Enterprise Linux, Debian, and Ubuntu, and
the honest answer to *what exactly is in the repository right now, and who put it
there* was a directory listing and a shell history. Making membership, immutable
generations, signing, and publication explicit turned that answer into state a
tool can show you — and turned a repository rebuild into an auditable release
boundary instead of a batch of files that appeared overnight.
