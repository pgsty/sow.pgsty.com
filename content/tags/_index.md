---
title: Tags
description: "Cross-cutting subjects — package formats, repository modes, and delivery mechanics — that recur across the documentation and the release notes."
---

Tags name the subject a page is about, independent of where it sits in the
navigation. `rpm`, `deb`, `yum`, and `apt` are package formats; `plain` and
`managed` are the two execution modes; `publish`, `generation`, `retention`,
and `transaction` are the mechanics of getting a repository to its readers.
